\begin{answer}
With the small dataset, low-degree polynomials ($k=1,2$) underfit — the curves are too simple to capture the structure in the data. At $k=5$ the fit is reasonable. For $k=10$ and especially $k=20$, the number of parameters approaches or exceeds the number of training examples, and the model \emph{overfits}: the curve passes through the training points almost exactly but oscillates wildly between them. Such a model has very poor generalization and would perform badly on unseen test data.
\end{answer}

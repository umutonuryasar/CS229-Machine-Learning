\begin{answer}
By Bayes' rule:
\[
p(t=1\mid y=1, x)
= \frac{p(y=1\mid t=1,x)\,p(t=1\mid x)}{p(y=1\mid x)}.
\]
Using the law of total probability for the denominator:
\[
p(y=1\mid x) = p(y=1\mid t=1,x)\,p(t=1\mid x) + \underbrace{p(y=1\mid t=0,x)}_{=0}\,p(t=0\mid x)
= \alpha\,p(t=1\mid x).
\]
Substituting back:
\[
p(t=1\mid y=1,x)
= \frac{\alpha\,p(t=1\mid x)}{\alpha\,p(t=1\mid x)} = 1. \qquad \blacksquare
\]
\end{answer}

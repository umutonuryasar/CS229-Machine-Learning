\begin{answer}
From the law of total probability (same computation as part (c)):
\[
p(y=1\mid x) = p(y=1\mid t=1,x)\,p(t=1\mid x) + p(y=1\mid t=0,x)\,p(t=0\mid x)
= \alpha\,p(t=1\mid x) + 0 = \alpha\,p(t=1\mid x).
\]
Rearranging:
\[
p(t=1\mid x) = \frac{1}{\alpha}\,p(y=1\mid x). \qquad \blacksquare
\]
\end{answer}

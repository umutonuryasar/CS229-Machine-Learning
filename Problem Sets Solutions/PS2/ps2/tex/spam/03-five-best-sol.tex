\begin{answer}
The five most indicative tokens for the spam class (ranked by $\log\frac{p(x_j=1\mid y=1)}{p(x_j=1\mid y=0)}$, highest first) are:

\begin{center}
\textbf{claim, won, prize, tone, urgent!}
\end{center}

These words are strongly associated with spam messages (prize claims, urgency cues) and rarely appear in legitimate SMS messages.

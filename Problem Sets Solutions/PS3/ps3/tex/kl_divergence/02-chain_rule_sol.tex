\begin{answer}
We expand $\KL(P(X,Y)\|Q(X,Y))$ using the chain rule of probability $P(x,y) = P(x)P(y|x)$:

\begin{align*}
\KL(P(X,Y)\|Q(X,Y))
&= \sum_{x,y} P(x,y) \log \frac{P(x,y)}{Q(x,y)} \\
&= \sum_{x,y} P(x,y) \log \frac{P(x)\,P(y|x)}{Q(x)\,Q(y|x)} \\
&= \sum_{x,y} P(x,y) \left[\log \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} + \log \frac{P(y|x)}{Q(y|x)}\right] \\
&= \sum_{x,y} P(x,y) \log \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} + \sum_{x,y} P(x,y) \log \frac{P(y|x)}{Q(y|x)}.
\end{align*}

For the first term, marginalizing over $y$:
$$\sum_{x,y} P(x,y)\log\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} = \sum_x \log\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\sum_y P(x,y) = \sum_x P(x)\log\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} = \KL(P(X)\|Q(X)).$$

For the second term:
\begin{align*}
\sum_{x,y} P(x,y)\log\frac{P(y|x)}{Q(y|x)}
&= \sum_x P(x) \sum_y P(y|x)\log\frac{P(y|x)}{Q(y|x)} \\
&= \KL(P(Y|X)\|Q(Y|X)).
\end{align*}

Combining both terms:
$$\KL(P(X,Y)\|Q(X,Y)) = \KL(P(X)\|Q(X)) + \KL(P(Y|X)\|Q(Y|X)). \qquad \square$$
\end{answer}

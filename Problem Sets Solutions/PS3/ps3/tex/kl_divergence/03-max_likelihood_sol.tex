\begin{answer}
We show $\arg\min_\theta \KL(\hat{P}\|P_\theta) = \arg\max_\theta \sum_{i=1}^\nexp \log P_\theta(\xsi)$.

Expanding the KL divergence:
\begin{align*}
\KL(\hat{P}\|P_\theta)
&= \sum_x \hat{P}(x)\log\frac{\hat{P}(x)}{P_\theta(x)} \\
&= \sum_x \hat{P}(x)\log \hat{P}(x) - \sum_x \hat{P}(x)\log P_\theta(x).
\end{align*}

The first term $\sum_x \hat{P}(x)\log\hat{P}(x) = -H(\hat{P})$ does not depend on $\theta$. Therefore:
$$\arg\min_\theta \KL(\hat{P}\|P_\theta) = \arg\max_\theta \sum_x \hat{P}(x)\log P_\theta(x).$$

Substituting $\hat{P}(x) = \frac{1}{\nexp}\sum_{i=1}^\nexp \mathbf{1}\{\xsi = x\}$:
\begin{align*}
\sum_x \hat{P}(x)\log P_\theta(x)
&= \sum_x \frac{1}{\nexp}\sum_{i=1}^\nexp \mathbf{1}\{\xsi = x\}\log P_\theta(x) \\
&= \frac{1}{\nexp}\sum_{i=1}^\nexp \log P_\theta(\xsi).
\end{align*}

Scaling by $\nexp > 0$ does not change the argmax, so:
$$\arg\min_\theta \KL(\hat{P}\|P_\theta) = \arg\max_\theta \sum_{i=1}^\nexp \log P_\theta(\xsi). \qquad \square$$
\end{answer}
